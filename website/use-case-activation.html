An example

From interest to action.

Define the key action for your own product, then build around it.

  1. Segment customers who have explored a feature but have not used it yet.
  2. Wait a short time to see whether they complete the action.
  3. Send a helpful email or push message to those who still need a hand.
  4. Stop the nudge path when the completion event arrives.
