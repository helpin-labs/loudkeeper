01 / Build

Turn a customer action into a clear path.

Start with an event or audience. Then add messages, waits, conditions, and branches in a visual builder that makes the full journey easy to follow.

  • Event-triggered and scheduled journeys
  • Waits, splits, and branching steps
  • Reusable templates and personalized content

02 / Understand

Find the people who need to hear from you.

Build audiences from customer attributes and events. Use what people have done to shape the next step, from a first welcome to a timely follow-up.

  • Customer profiles and event history
  • Segments based on attributes and behavior
  • Audience rules inside journeys

03 / Reach

Choose the channel that fits the moment.

Keep messages connected as a journey moves between email, SMS, push, and webhooks. Use templates and customer data to make each touchpoint more relevant.

  • Email, SMS, push, and webhook steps
  • Liquid-based message personalization
  • Delivery and engagement events
